### The short version

For seven years I've been accountable for the things governance is actually about: compliance, records, people, and operational risk, while running a multi-brand business in Karnataka.

- **Compliance and records:** tax compliance and book-keeping, owned end to end
- **People:** hiring, managing, and retaining staff
- **Third parties:** vendors and events coordinated under real accountability
- **Operations:** agentic AI workflows built into the day-to-day

I know what a control costs a business, not only what the framework says it should do. A finding doesn't matter because a tool flagged it. It matters for what it would cost the business and what it takes to actually close it.

### Flagship project

#### [ISO 27001 & DPDP Risk Assessment](https://github.com/akshaykumarph/ISO27001-DPDP-Risk-Assessment)

<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/ISO_27001%3A2022-1F3864?style=flat-square" alt="ISO 27001:2022"> <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/DPDP_Act-1F3864?style=flat-square" alt="DPDP Act"> <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Status-Remediation_in_progress-2E7D32?style=flat-square" alt="Status: remediation in progress">

A first-pass GRC gap analysis of a real small business (anonymised), mapped to ISO 27001:2022 and India's DPDP Act.

| Deliverable | What it contains |
|---|---|
| **Data inventory** | What the business holds, where it lives, and who touches it |
| **Risk register** | Scored risks, ranked by business impact |
| **Statement of Applicability** | All 93 Annex A controls, with justified exclusions |
| **Remediation roadmap** | Phased plan, now being executed from Open to Closed |

### Technical depth

The hands-on work that keeps the governance honest.

| Project | Domain | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| **[AI-Augmented SOC Lab](https://github.com/akshaykumarph/AI-Augmented-SOC-Lab)** | Detection and response | Three-VM SOC: Splunk, Elastic ML, LogAI, OpenCTI, Shuffle SOAR. Attack simulation, cross-source threat hunting, MITRE ATT&CK/D3FEND mapping, and a prioritised gap analysis. |
| **[bee-box VAPT](https://github.com/akshaykumarph/Beebox-VAPT)** | Penetration testing | Full PTES engagement: 10 findings, SNMP enumeration, anonymous FTP exploitation, and a remediation applied *and verified* by re-running the attack. |
| **[Breach at the Perimeter](https://github.com/akshaykumarph/Breach-at-the-Perimeter)** | Detection evasion | Metasploit and Nikto: default, spoofed, and mutated scans compared to show how request obfuscation defeats signature-based detection. |
| **[VulnVMA VAPT](https://github.com/akshaykumarph/VulnVMA-VAPT)** | Vulnerability assessment | OWASP-mapped findings across misconfiguration, information disclosure, and access control, with remediation guidance. |
